About the Board

Our History

The Sikkim Board of School Education (SBSE) was established in 1978 under the Sikkim Board of School Education Act, 1978 (Act No. 19 of 1978). The Board was constituted to regulate and improve the standards of school education in the State of Sikkim.

Since its inception, the Board has been committed to providing quality education and conducting fair examinations at the school level. Over the years, SBSE has grown to become the apex body for school education in the state.

The Board operates under the guidance of the Government of Sikkim and works towards implementing educational policies and reforms at the school level.

Our Objectives & Functions

  • Prescribing curriculum and syllabi for school education
  • Developing and prescribing textbooks and instructional materials
  • Conducting Secondary (SSLC) and Higher Secondary (HSC) examinations
  • Granting recognition to schools in the state
  • Registering teachers and maintaining teacher records
  • Advising the State Government on matters related to school education
  • Ensuring quality standards in education across all affiliated schools
  • Promoting academic excellence and holistic development of students

Organizational Structure

Position Role
President Head of the Board, responsible for overall governance and policy decisions
Secretary Chief Executive Officer responsible for day-to-day administration
Members Educators and officials appointed to advise on academic matters
Head of Institution Heads of affiliated educational institutions
Employees Full-time and part-time staff assisting in Board operations
Registered Teachers Teachers registered with the Board as per regulations
