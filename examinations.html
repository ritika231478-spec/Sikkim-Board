Examination Guidelines

Guideline Details
Admit Card Students must carry their admit card to the examination hall. No student will be allowed to appear without a valid admit card.
Reporting Time Students must reach the examination center at least 30 minutes before the scheduled start time.
Prohibited Items Mobile phones, electronic devices, calculators, and any study material are strictly prohibited in the examination hall.
Stationery Students must bring their own pens, pencils, erasers, and other required stationery items.
Malpractice Any form of malpractice or unfair means will result in immediate disqualification and may lead to further disciplinary action.

About SBSE

The Sikkim Board of School Education was established in 1978 under Act No. 19 of 1978 to regulate and improve school education in the state.
